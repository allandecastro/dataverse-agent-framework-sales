# Worked examples

Two scorecards that show where the lines are. Evidence excerpts are verbatim and short.
Record ids here are illustrative. There is no "role" field - Economic buyer and Champion are
inferred from notes and job titles.

## Example 1 - a well-qualified deal

Context: a data-platform opportunity at Propose, amount 140,000. A discovery note names who
owns the decision and quantifies the pain; a timing note sets a board deadline; the buyer has
engaged three times in the last month.

- Metrics: **Confirmed** - "costs the data team about 10 hours each week in manual reprocessing."
- Economic buyer: **Confirmed** - "Omar owns this decision", and Omar is the Head of Data.
- Decision criteria: **Partial** - a "Proposal questions" call shows evaluation, but the
  criteria themselves are not written down.
- Decision process: **Confirmed** - "board review at the end of next month", "finalise
  commercials this month."
- Paper process: **Partial** - stage is Propose with a close date in three weeks, but no
  contract or legal step is recorded yet.
- Identify pain: **Confirmed** - "their pipeline breaks weekly."
- Champion: **Confirmed** - Omar engaged three times in a month, "close alignment" on commercials.
- Competition: **Missing** - no competitor is named in any record.
- Score: 5/8. Strong on pain, buyer, and process; the gaps are written criteria and the paper trail.

## Example 2 - an under-qualified deal

Context: a replatforming opportunity at Propose, amount 90,000. Only an operational contact has
engaged. No decision owner, no metric, no dates beyond the seller's close date.

- Metrics: **Missing** - no record quantifies the impact.
- Economic buyer: **Missing** - no note says who owns the decision; only a developer has engaged.
- Decision criteria: **Missing** - nothing records how they will evaluate.
- Decision process: **Missing** - no customer-owned dates or steps.
- Paper process: **Partial** - a stage and a close date exist, but the close date is the seller's.
- Identify pain: **Partial** - "the team wants to modernise" with no specific problem.
- Champion: **Missing** - one operational contact, no advocacy in the notes.
- Competition: **Missing** - none named.
- Score: 0/8 confirmed. The deal sits at Propose but almost nothing supports it - qualify the
  buyer and the pain first.
