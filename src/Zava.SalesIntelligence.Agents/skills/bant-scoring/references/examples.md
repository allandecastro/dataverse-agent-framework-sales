# Worked examples

Four assessments that show where the lines are. The fourth refuses to conclude,
which is a valid and expected outcome. Evidence excerpts are verbatim and short.
Record ids here are illustrative.

## Example 1 - ReadyToClose

Context: Trey Research, opportunity at Propose, amount 260,000. The VP Finance
approved the budget in writing, a specific need is documented with a metric, and
the customer owns a go-live date. Activity rose over the last month.

- Budget: **Confirmed** - "VP Finance approved the 260k for this fiscal year."
- Authority: **Confirmed** - VP Finance is a contact and appears in a meeting.
- Need: **Confirmed** - "manual reconciliation costs the team 15 hours a week."
- Timing: **Confirmed** - "must be live before the March audit."
- Score: 4. Archetype: **ReadyToClose**. Confidence: High.
- Rationale: Every axis is backed by a distinct record and the customer owns the
  date. Push for signature this month.

## Example 2 - Slipping

Context: Contoso Logistics, opportunity at Propose, amount 400,000. A meeting note
from three weeks ago names a competitor. The close date has moved twice. The
economic buyer has not been active for six weeks.

- Budget: **Partial** - a figure was discussed but never validated in writing.
- Authority: **Partial** - the buyer is named but has gone quiet.
- Need: **Confirmed** - "current provider misses SLAs twice a month."
- Timing: **Missing** - the close date is the seller's, and it keeps moving.
- Score: 1. Archetype: **Slipping**. Confidence: Medium.
- Rationale: A competitor is named within 60 days and the close date has moved
  twice, so the deal is being lost despite the Propose stage. Re-engage the buyer
  before the competitor sets the terms.

## Example 3 - AtRisk

Context: Fourth Coffee, opportunity at Propose, amount 180,000. Only operational
contacts have engaged. No budget is confirmed and no sponsor is identified. No
competitor is named and the close date has not moved.

- Budget: **Missing** - no record mentions money on the customer side.
- Authority: **Missing** - all known contacts are operational.
- Need: **Partial** - "the team liked the demo" with no specific pain.
- Timing: **Missing** - the close date is the only date.
- Score: 0. Archetype: **AtRisk**. Confidence: Medium.
- Rationale: The deal sits at Propose but nothing supports that stage, and no
  competitor is in play, so it is under-qualified rather than slipping. Find an
  economic buyer before advancing.

## Example 4 - InsufficientSignal (refuses to conclude)

Context: Wide World Importers, opportunity at Qualify, amount 50,000. One intro
email and a single operational contact. Nothing else.

- Budget: **Missing** - no record mentions money.
- Authority: **Missing** - one operational contact, no buying process.
- Need: **Missing** - nothing describes what they are trying to solve.
- Timing: **Missing** - only the close date exists.
- Score: 0. Fewer than two axes are documented.
- Archetype: none. Confidence: **InsufficientSignal**.
- Rationale: There is not enough evidence to judge this opportunity. Do not assign
  an archetype. Gather more before concluding.
